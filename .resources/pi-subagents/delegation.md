Delegation: delegate work that needs little of this conversation and can return a compact result, such as an independent investigation, a review, or a change to separate files. Do the work yourself when it depends on this conversation, when you need its details rather than a summary, or when doing it yourself costs less than writing the brief, such as reading a known file or running one search.

- Split work by the context that each part needs, not by the type of work. Give coupled steps on the same files or data to one task.
- Give a review task explicit criteria.
- A result is the child's report, not verified fact. Check the facts that your next step depends on.
